# Orbit — approved refined V2 production lessons

## Scope and precedence
The creator approved Vision Proxy refined V2's storyboarding, animation, transition flow, typography and picture quality. For the Orbit preset, this supersedes conflicting legacy font/palette defaults, not safety, source rights, factual accuracy or publishing permissions. Other brands remain configurable. Apply refinements to episode copies, not accepted originals.

## Audit before changing
Separate successful structure from weak execution. Preserve approved scene order, mode switches, horizontal/vertical pane flow, narration and timing when asked to polish. Do not fully restructure a successful edit. Freeze scene boundaries/master transition properties/audio and mechanically compare before and after. Preserve the canonical robot silhouette, control hierarchy and construction; more metallic texture is not automatically better.

## Typography
Approved Orbit pairing: Instrument Sans Medium/SemiBold for UI, headlines and captions; selective Cormorant Garamond SemiBold Italic for editorial keywords. Geist remains a legacy option, not exclusive. Use sentence case; preserve actual acronyms such as OCR, CPU, MIT and API. Obtain authoritative font files, retain license/OFL and provenance, verify intended variable-font instances and family/style names. Measure actual glyph widths, not character-count estimates. Test the longest real line.

## Coordinated color and surfaces
Keep approved Black_White compositions recognizable. Coordinate graphite/pearl panel gradients and neutral high-contrast text with the chosen background. Use muted gray-green for semantic active states; reserve bright cyan mainly for the robot. Avoid rainbow labels and arbitrary colored badges. Inspect active/inactive and entering/settled states on dark and light scenes. A PNG surface is not a native editable gradient effect; describe implementation honestly.

## Staged words paired with performance
Use separate measured word layers with short staggered opacity/translation and subtle critically damped scale settling, synchronized to spoken starts. Keep compact grouped captions stable, with restrained active-word pills and small entrance movement. Pair meaningful narration cues with independent hand extension/rotation, pointing, eyelines and neck responses. Avoid constant idle noise or bouncing every syllable. Preserve good pane transitions; inspect horizontal and vertical midframes for gaps, ghost overlap and timing discontinuities. Do not replace good transitions with gratuitous flashes.

## UI must explain, not decorate
Show legible illustrative fields/button strings rather than permanent skeletons. Stage navigation selection, toggle thumb travel, option changes and pressed-button feedback in sync with the explanation. Keep bounding boxes, scan effects and progress indicators off labels. Delta-cache progress belongs only to the changed region; cached regions stay stable. Clearly label illustrative coordinates/output. Use exact semantic roles/IDs rather than substring style matching (STATE must not accidentally match STAT).

## Relevant assets
Use real relevant marks when discussed: Qwen, DeepSeek, GitHub, NVIDIA, OpenAI. Time model chips to the spoken model. Record authoritative sources and trademark ownership; do not imply endorsement. Never invent a missing Mercury logo. Match monochrome asset size and contrast to the surface. Do not redistribute assets without applicable rights.

## QA, rendering and delivery
Inspect every scene individually, longest lines, active states, caption boundaries, hand extremes and encoded transition samples. Fix clipping, unreadable metadata, text insets and effects crossing labels. Keep source/revised timing comparisons, asset provenance, licenses, generator scripts and limitations. Validate dimensions, fps, duration, audio, frame count where available, full decode, byte size and SHA256. Audio-level checks do not replace listening/caption review. Use bounded resumable chunks and capacity-appropriate concurrency; verify chunks before concatenating and mux audio once.

## Honest native parity and access limits
The approved 1080x1920, 60fps, 180-second V2 preview was rendered with a local SVG adaptation after remote access was blocked. Revised native EffectCraft reopening/render parity was NOT verified. Do not turn visual approval into a false native-reopen claim. Future delivery must verify native parity or prominently retain this limitation. Do not bypass access blocks through encoded commands or alternate routes; reconnect through supported authentication.

## Publishing
Require clear destination/account approval and publish the exact approved export. Include the real project repository and a few familiar relevant hashtags; never promise reach. Send literal # characters rather than percent-encoded hashtag text. Do not invent media URLs. Verify processing, final caption and returned published permalink before declaring success. Never put credentials, local machine manifests, private narration or unrelated footage into a public skill repository.
